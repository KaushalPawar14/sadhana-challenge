# Academic calendar research · 2026–27

Checked on 6 August 2026. Dates enter the CRM as candidates and remain pending until a FOLK guide approves that they apply to the relevant student or cohort.

## SVNIT

The official 2026–27 calendar is available at [SVNIT Academic Calendar 2026–27](https://www.svnit.ac.in/Data/Notice/2025/June/Academic%20Calendar%202026-27.pdf). Candidate dates seeded into Supabase include:

- teaching begins 27 July 2026;
- autumn mid-semester examinations 21–26 September 2026;
- Diwali break 9–13 November 2026;
- autumn end-semester examinations 23–30 November 2026;
- UG vacation 14 December 2026–1 January 2027;
- spring teaching begins 4 January 2027;
- spring mid-semester examinations 8–15 March 2027;
- spring end-semester examinations 26 April–1 May 2027;
- project/internship examinations 3–7 May 2027; and
- UG vacation begins 10 May 2027.

The planner should derive a one-week cooldown before examination windows. First-year admission or orientation variations must be confirmed separately.

## Local colleges

Each student may keep a college name and an optional institution calendar. The system should search an official college source first, then ask the assigned guide for tentative dates. No calendar is attached automatically without guide approval.
